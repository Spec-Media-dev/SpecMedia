1. Purpose and authority
This document converts the supplied SEO workbook into an executable development baseline for rebuilding SPEC Media. It defines what developers must build, what SEO/content teams must supply, and how acceptance will be tested.
Primary markets: Dubai first, UAE second, then selected GCC/MENA demand. Egypt may be represented operationally without displacing the UAE commercial positioning.
Primary audiences: founders, CEOs, marketing leaders and business owners seeking measurable growth, web, SEO, paid media, social, branding, content production or AI marketing services.
Primary conversion: qualified consultation, audit, proposal or campaign request captured with source and service intent.
The SEO workbook is the source for route priorities, keywords, page briefs and historical audit evidence. Approved business positioning, legal copy and final design supersede placeholders.

4.2 Route rules
URL-01 — Canonical style [MUST]
Use lowercase, hyphen-separated URLs. Select one canonical hostname (recommended: https://spec-media.com) and one trailing-slash policy, then enforce it consistently.
URL-02 — No route aliases [MUST]
/home must redirect to /. Do not expose duplicate /case-study and /portfolio structures unless each has distinct approved purpose.
URL-03 — Real links [MUST]
Primary navigation, cards, breadcrumbs and contextual links must use crawlable HTML anchors to canonical URLs; essential discovery must not depend on JavaScript click handlers.
URL-04 — Language strategy [MUST]
If Arabic launches, use a complete /ar/ tree with localized metadata/content and reciprocal hreflang. Never publish thin auto-translated duplicates.
5. Required template system
6. Commercial landing-page backlog
The following routes are explicitly supported by the SEO workbook. P1 pages launch first; P2/P3 follow after content approval. Similar keywords do not justify duplicate thin pages.
LAND-01 — Intent ownership [MUST]
Each landing page owns one primary commercial intent and has unique copy, proof, FAQs, metadata and internal links. Pages must not be programmatic location swaps.
LAND-02 — Publication gate [MUST]
A route is indexable only when its content, proof, metadata, structured data and CTA are complete. Draft/placeholder pages remain unpublished or noindex outside sitemaps.
7. CMS and data model
CMS-01 — Structured editing [MUST]
Editors choose structured sections/components; do not store whole pages as uncontrolled HTML blobs.
CMS-02 — Workflow [MUST]
Support Draft → Review → Approved → Scheduled/Published → Archived with role restrictions and revision history.
CMS-03 — Validation [MUST]
Block publication when required H1, title, description, canonical, CTA, image alt text or route ownership is missing/invalid.
8. Lead-generation and qualification system
Every high-intent page must end in a contextual conversion, not the same generic “Contact Us” button everywhere.
LEAD-01 — Attribution [MUST]
Persist landing page, referrer, first/last UTM, campaign IDs where available, language, form type, service, market, consent version and submission timestamp.
LEAD-02 — Reliability [MUST]
Validate server-side, rate-limit, use spam controls, prevent duplicate retry submissions and return clear success/error states.
LEAD-03 — Privacy [MUST]
Do not send sensitive form data to analytics. Uploaded files must be private, scanned, access-controlled and excluded from public storage/indexing.
LEAD-04 — CRM handoff [MUST]
Use an API/webhook or export with idempotency and delivery status. Failure must enter a retry queue and alert an administrator; no lead may disappear silently.
9. Case studies, proof and E-E-A-T
PROOF-01 — Evidence hierarchy [MUST]
Case studies must describe verified challenge, actions and outcomes. Portfolio imagery alone is not a case study.
PROOF-02 — Claims [MUST]
Metrics and testimonials require internal source/approval. Do not publish invented percentages, client logos or confidential results.
PROOF-03 — Relationships [MUST]
Each case study links to its services, sector/market and relevant insights; each service page surfaces approved related cases.
PROOF-04 — Media accessibility [MUST]
Galleries include descriptive alt text/captions where meaningful, correct dimensions and responsive loading. Decorative assets use empty alt text.
10. Technical SEO contract
SEO-01 — Metadata safety [MUST]
Never output placeholders, [object Object], blank canonicals, duplicated global descriptions or unpublished CMS values.
SEO-02 — HTTP behavior [MUST]
200 only for valid pages; 301 for permanent migration; 404/410 for missing/removed content. Soft 404s and redirect chains fail acceptance.
11. Migration and redirect requirements
Build a complete old-to-new URL inventory using the supplied “All URLs Master”, GSC exports, database backup, analytics and a fresh crawl where possible.
Resolve host variants (http/https and www/non-www) to one canonical host with a single 301 hop.
Redirect /home to / and consolidate overlapping portfolio/case-study routes only after a signed mapping.
Replace encoded spaces and uppercase paths with lowercase hyphenated slugs, preserving value through exact 301 mappings.
Update all internal links to final canonical 200 URLs rather than relying on redirects.
Retain migration monitoring for at least 30/60/90 days: 404s, redirect loops, sitemap processing, indexation and traffic.
MIG-01 — Redirect register [MUST]
No bulk redirect to the homepage. Every high-value historical URL maps to the closest relevant successor or intentionally returns 410 when no replacement exists.
